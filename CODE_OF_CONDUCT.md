# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ExtensionMesh is committed to an open, professional and welcoming project
environment. Participation should be possible regardless of experience,
background, identity, employer or commercial interest.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining precise about technical concerns.
- Welcome questions and explain project context when practical.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing sensitive information.
- Accept maintainer decisions once the reasoning has been documented, while
  remaining free to propose a future change constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or infrastructure.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Abusing project access or identity for personal or commercial leverage.
- Misrepresenting a modified or external project as officially endorsed by
  ExtensionMesh.

## Scope

This code applies in ExtensionMesh repositories, project communication spaces
and public situations where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to an ExtensionMesh organization maintainer. If private
contact is needed, contact an organization owner through their public GitHub
profile and request a private communication channel without including sensitive
details publicly.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and impartially as practical. Maintainers
who are directly involved in a report should not lead its resolution.

## Enforcement

Responses may include clarification, a warning, temporary participation limits,
removal of project access or a permanent ban. The response should be
proportionate to the behavior, its impact and any repeated pattern.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
